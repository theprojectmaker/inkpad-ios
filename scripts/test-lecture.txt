Okay everyone, today we are learning about slope. Slope is defined as rise over run. Remember, the formula for slope is y two minus y one, over x two minus x one. For example, if a line goes through the points one, two and three, six, the rise is four and the run is two. So the slope is two. A positive slope means the line goes up from left to right. A negative slope means the line goes down from left to right. Important: a horizontal line has a slope of zero. A vertical line has an undefined slope.
